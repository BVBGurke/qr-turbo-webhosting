HOW IT WORKS

Three simple steps.

01Choose a file

Open Send and choose a file on your computer.

02Start playback

Play four QR codes at 30 FPS, ideally in fullscreen.

03Scan with your camera

Open the Camera receiver on your phone, keep all four codes in view, and save the recovered file.

i
How fast is it?

BeamFerry documents a computer-to-computer reference test of approximately 425 KB/s via screen capture. Phone cameras can be much slower. The stable starting point is four codes at 30 FPS; higher FPS may reduce reliability.
